# Options research: price the forecast before choosing the contract

User direction on 2026-09-16: consider trading options instead of intraday stocks. Recommendation: make options an independent research track and a competing expression of validated forecasts. Do not mechanically turn the failed SPY continuation signal into call/put purchases.

The original candidate is a **volatility-persistence mismatch**: distinguish a temporary burst of realized movement from a sustained change, then ask whether available option prices compensate us for the projected distribution, time decay, surface changes and execution costs. This is a falsifiable hypothesis, not a discovered edge. GARCH supplies a baseline for conditional variance; it does not establish that options are mispriced. The challenger must beat simple volatility and directional baselines using causally available inputs, then beat stock exposure and staying flat after costs at a comparable declared risk budget.

For the first implementation, compare a purchased call/put with a debit vertical and WAIT. Restrict to standard contracts, bounded premium outlay, intraday exits before expiration and a single underlying. Do not implement naked short options, automatic 0DTE selection, or an expiration-only payoff approximation for intraday marks. Before querying research outcomes, freeze underlying, entry schedule, maturities, strike rule, holding period, maximum premium, spread/staleness filters, surface model, sample phases and evaluation budget in a separate executable plan. Those choices are not frozen by this design note and there is no options backtest yet.

The numerical model should produce a joint distribution over underlying return and changes in implied volatility, including residual uncertainty. At each permitted action, revalue the actual contract across that distribution, apply executable quote costs, and compare expected outcomes and tail exposure. Keep physical forecasts distinct from risk-neutral pricing. A population/world simulation that generates attractive payoffs without reproducing observed joint behavior is not sufficient evidence. Validate distribution forecasts and actual policy returns separately, including the rule that selects a contract.

The current funded-long stock ledger does not provide these features.

## Decision

Options deserve research because a useful prediction about volatility or the shape of future outcomes can have value independently of a simple directional bet. They are not an approved replacement until their own evidence and lifecycle accounting exist. If the objective is to accelerate a small account, leverage alone increases dispersion; it is not evidence of positive expected returns.

Sources consulted: [OIC implied volatility overview](https://www.optionseducation.org/videolibrary/what-is-implied-volatility), [OIC bull call spread](https://www.optionseducation.org/strategies/all-strategies/bull-call-spread-debit-call-spread), and the connected provider's historical quotes and reference-contract endpoint documentation. Implied volatility affects option valuation; a debit spread caps the textbook terminal payoff but does not remove operational or assignment risks. Provider access was checked directly rather than inferred from documentation.
